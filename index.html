What it does

An edge-to-cloud telemetry pipeline for electronic target ranges.

🎯

Live shot capture

Tails the SIUS CommService log without locking it, extracting each _SHOT the instant it lands.

🧭

Clock-face direction

Converts X/Y impact coordinates into a coach-friendly clock direction (e.g. “2:00 high-right”).

📊

Series & 10x scoring

Groups shots into series of 10 with decimal and ISSF-integer totals and inner-ten counts.

🔌

Resilient by design

Decoupled worker pool with automatic date-stamped CSV fallback if the network drops.
